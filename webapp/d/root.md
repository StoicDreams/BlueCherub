<h2>Make your drawings move. Free.</h2>
<p>Blue Cherub is a free animation studio that runs in your browser. Bring an SVG drawing, rig it, and animate it on a timeline. There's no paywall, no premium tier, and no catch. The cherub is the only one here who causes trouble.</p>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/rig-layers.png" alt="The Rig tab, with the Blue Cherub drawing and its layers listed beside it">
<h3>Rig any drawing</h3>
<p>Import any SVG. Most drawings aren't built for animation, so the studio assumes yours isn't either. Pick shapes on the drawing or in the layers, group and ungroup them without anything moving, clean up groups that do nothing, and name the pieces.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/rig-pivots.png" video="/img/screenshots/studio/rig-pivots.mp4" alt="A selected arm with its pivot dot at the shoulder, and lines joining each part to the part it hangs from" reverse>
<h3>Pivots and attachments</h3>
<p>Mark the pieces that move as parts. Drag each part's pivot to the joint it turns around, and attach parts to each other, so a hand follows the arm and the arm follows the body. Press Test to watch a part swing before you animate anything.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/rig-swaps.png" video="/img/screenshots/studio/rig-swaps.mp4" alt="The eyes part with two swap drawings listed: eyes open and eyes closed">
<h3>Swap drawings</h3>
<p>Some things don't bend, they change. Give a part alternate drawings, like eyes open and closed, or a smile and a scream, and switch between them on any frame.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-timeline.png" video="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-timeline.mp4" alt="The Animate tab, with a timeline of keys for each part below the drawing" reverse>
<h3>Animate on a timeline</h3>
<p>Every part gets a lane of keys. Scrub to a frame, pose the character, and each change becomes a key. Choose how motion eases between keys, set the frame rate and length, and animate on twos for that hand-drawn feel.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-pose.png" video="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-pose.mp4" alt="The rotate tool aiming Blue Cherub's bow arm, with a ring around its pivot">
<h3>Pose by hand</h3>
<p>Drag a part to move it, or switch to Rotate and swing it around its pivot. Prefer numbers? Type the position, angle, scale, or opacity. Undo is never more than a Ctrl+Z away.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-library.png" video="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-library.mp4" alt="The library with a saved pose called Aim and a saved action called Flap" reverse>
<h3>Poses and actions you can reuse</h3>
<p>Save a pose, then drop it onto any frame. Save a stretch of motion as an action, like a wing flap, and insert it anywhere, as many times as you like. The scene grows to fit.</p>
</app-feature>

<app-feature src="/img/screenshots/studio/animate-export.png" alt="The export panel with MP4 video chosen at 720p, showing the size and length">
<h3>Export video or frames</h3>
<p>Export an MP4 video, ready for YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, or every frame as a PNG with a transparent background if you like. Pick the size, from 360p to 4K. It all happens in your browser, so your work never leaves your computer.</p>
</app-feature>
